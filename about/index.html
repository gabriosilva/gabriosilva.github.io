About

I’m Gabrio Lina, a software engineer at Morgan Stanley in São Paulo. I build software for trade allocation, payments and digital signing, across Java, C# and Angular, with global teams.

I studied Computer Science at Inteli, where every term ended with a working product for an industry partner: a flight route planner, a compiler for therapy activities, a reinforcement-learning agent for trade matching, and more. Before that I worked as a site reliability engineer, and before that I made films.

These days I work on backend and full-stack systems and on AI agent tooling. I’m learning C++ by writing neural networks from scratch.
